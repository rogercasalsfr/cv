About

I am a PhD candidate in Bioinformatics at the Universitat de Vic – Universitat Central de Catalunya (UVic-UCC), supervised by Jordi Villà-Freixa and Adrián López García de Lomana. I integrate transcriptomic, epigenomic, metabolic and T-cell receptor repertoire data to study how T and NKT cells respond to vaccination, chronic infection, cancer and immunotherapy.

My work centres on gene regulatory network inference, trajectory analysis and perturbation modelling, and I am increasingly using deep learning and biological foundation models on large perturbational datasets. The long-term aim is to combine mechanistic systems biology with modern AI to produce interpretable hypotheses about immune control, dysfunction and therapeutic response.

PhD defence expected July 2027.

Projects

Boolyne

In development

PhD method · UVic-UCC

A Boolean gene regulatory network framework for mechanistic modelling of cell-state transitions. Networks inferred from single-cell multi-omic data are constrained to reproduce the cell states and transitions actually observed, so that in silico perturbations can be read mechanistically.

  • Boolean networks
  • GRN inference
  • Perturbations

Presented at JBI 2025, scverse 2025 (Stanford) and ECCB 2026.

T-cell reprogramming under PD-1 blockade

PhD · with L. Nonell, J. Villà-Freixa, A. López García de Lomana

Trajectory inference and gene regulatory network analysis of 25,581 tumour-infiltrating T cells from squamous cell carcinoma, before and after anti-PD-1. Resolves memory-to-exhaustion trajectories driven by EOMES and TCF7, Tpex vs Tex rewiring, and enhanced CXCL13⁺ Tfh responses.

  • Trajectory inference
  • SCENIC
  • TCR clonotypes
Preprint Analysis code Reproducible protocol

GRETA: benchmarking multimodal GRN inference

Saez-Rodriguez group, Heidelberg · 2024

A modular Snakemake framework to compare methods that infer gene regulatory networks from paired and unpaired single-cell RNA + ATAC data. Inferred networks proved highly sensitive to method choices and struggled to capture causal effects from perturbation assays.

  • Benchmark
  • scRNA + scATAC
  • Snakemake
Preprint saezlab/greta

Therapeutic HIV vaccination at single-cell resolution

Ongoing

IrsiCaixa · group of Christian Brander

Single-cell transcriptomic and epigenomic analysis of BCN03, a therapeutic HIV vaccine trial, to characterise vaccine-induced changes in gene regulation. T- and NKT-cell state annotation is integrated with TCR repertoire analysis to compare clonotype usage between people with HIV and healthy controls.

  • scRNA + scATAC
  • TCR repertoire
  • NKT cells

Physics-informed neural networks for the repressilator

with B. Casajuana, A. López García de Lomana, P. Martí-Puig, J. Villà-Freixa · 2026

How reliably can PINNs recover kinetic parameters of a synthetic genetic oscillator? Trajectories are reconstructed well, but parameter recovery is fragile under noise, sparse sampling, unobserved repressors and poor initial guesses, especially in the oscillatory regime.

  • PINNs
  • ODE inverse problems
  • Dynamical systems

scHLA-demux

Workflow · Snakemake

Donor demultiplexing of pooled 10x single-cell data from raw FASTQs: Cell Ranger multi, genotype-based clustering with souporcell, HLA typing of each cluster with arcasHLA, and matching back to known donor HLA alleles.

Expression-informed metabolic modelling

Biomedical Center, University of Iceland · 2025

Context-specific genome-scale metabolic models built from RNA-seq with rFASTCORMICS, constrained with Seahorse measurements and analysed with COBRApy, to predict metabolic responses to treatment.

  • Constraint-based models

SECUELAS hackathon: lung recovery after hospitalisation

Team project, Group 9 · with I. Carbó, M. Navarro, G. Ruiz · 2026

A hierarchical Bayesian model that predicts, from the 3-month follow-up visit, whether lung diffusing capacity (DLCO) will recover by 6 months and whether the patient will return, across three post-COVID and post-ICU cohorts. Validated on unseen hospitals and served through a web app.
